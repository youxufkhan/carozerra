About Carozerra

Some Pioneer car stereos had tiny animated worlds in their OEL displays: dolphins, mountains, racing cars, and more. Custom animations were stored in .lkd files.

Carozerra decodes those files and plays them on a Pioneer DEH-P7600MP faceplate in your browser. Choose a clip from the collection, or open files from your own discs.

You can adjust the speed, step through frames, and export an animation as GIF or WebM. Files you open are processed on your device.

The Android and desktop apps are also available. Android and Windows builds are beta releases.
